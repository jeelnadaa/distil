    Interactive learning

    Learn it by touching it.

    When a concept moves, Distil builds something you can move: plots, simulations, step-through derivations, small apps. Each one is tested before you see it.

    How much?

    Visuals only when a concept is dynamic, spatial or a process.

    Four ways to study

    One app. No tab-hopping.

    1. 01

      Plan

      Units, lessons and steps in dependency order. Give it an exam date and it becomes a schedule that rebalances when life happens.

    2. 02

      Teach

      It finds where your understanding ends, then builds from there, one step at a time, with a check after every step.

    3. 03

      Practice

      Problem sets and timed mock exams in your course's style. Solutions and rubrics are written before you answer. Photograph handwritten work.

    4. 04

      Ask

      Quick answers grounded in your own material, with the page each one came from.

    Retention

    Standing on shoulders

    Distil's teaching loop (probe, plan, teach one step, check, practise) and its knowledge graph build on ideas from LearnHarness. Distil is an independent implementation that adds material ingestion, an enforced coverage ledger, subject workspaces, schedules, flashcards and mock exams.
