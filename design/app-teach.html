Teaching · 6 steps

One rotation instead of two waves

You already hold Euler's identity: ejθ = cos θ + j sin θ. Read it as a point going round the unit circle. Its shadow on the real axis is a cosine; its shadow on the imaginary axis is a sine.

So instead of tracking a sine and a cosine at every frequency, we track one spinning arrow. A frequency nω₀ is just an arrow spinning n times faster than the slowest one.

Key ideaA periodic signal is a sum of arrows spinning at whole-number multiples of one base speed, ω₀ = 2π / T.

Why a square wave has only odd harmonics

Flip the second half of a square wave upside down and slide it back half a period, and you get the first half exactly. Any even harmonic would break that symmetry, so its coefficient has to be zero.
