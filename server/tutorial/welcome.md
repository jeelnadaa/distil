Welcome to **Distil**. I'm the guide: ask me anything about how Distil works, here, any time. Here's the whole app in five minutes.

**1. Connect a model.** Add a key in [Settings › Providers](#/settings/providers), then choose a *strong* model (teaching, plans, exams) and a *cheap* one (bulk work) in [Settings › Models](#/settings/models). Google AI Studio has a free tier.

**2. Make a folder for a subject** with the folder button next to *Library* in the sidebar, and open it.

**3. Add your material** on the **Materials** tab: PDFs, slides, documents, photos of notes, web pages or YouTube videos. No material? **Research a topic** writes a syllabus for you.

**4. Distil it.** The **Distil** button reads everything and lists every learnable item on the **Ledger** tab, each linked to where it came from. It shows the token cost first.

**5. Plan it.** On the **Plan** tab, describe your goal in one sentence (exam, date, level). The plan covers every item, in order, with a schedule to your deadline.

**6. Learn it.** Open a lesson on the plan and press **Start lesson**. Each step is taught from your material with citations, followed by a check; a wrong answer gets explained another way.

**7. Test yourself.** The **Practice** tab writes practice sets and timed mock exams with mark schemes, and marks your answers, handwriting included.

**8. Keep it.** Lessons and missed questions become flashcards. [Review](#/review) brings each one back just before you'd forget it; the **Progress** tab shows mastery and a knowledge graph.

**Every day,** start at [Today](#/today): it says what to do next and why.

Also good to know: [Usage](#/usage) shows where your tokens go, caps live in [Settings › Limits](#/settings/limits), and **?** shows every keyboard shortcut.
